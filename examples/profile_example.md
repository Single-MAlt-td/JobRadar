# Example JobRadar AI Profile

> This is a fictional example profile. It is not the maintainer's real profile.

This profile is intended to evaluate vacancies not only by skill overlap, but by long-term technical fit, day-to-day work, motivation, company/domain fit and practical constraints.

The AI should not reward buzzword matches by themselves. It should focus on what the person would actually work on every day and whether that work is likely to remain motivating.

---

# Professional background

- Physicist with approximately 5 years of professional experience.
- Main background: software development for coffee machines.
- Strong interest in coffee-machine technology and improving coffee preparation.
- Engineering-oriented rather than product-management-oriented.
- Most recent job title: Coffee Machine Software Engineer.

---

# Hard exclusions

## Food industry

- No food-industry roles except work directly related to coffee or coffee machines.
- No animal-industry roles.

## Contract model and seniority

- Employee leasing / temporary agency work (Arbeitnehmerüberlassung / ANÜ) is strongly negative and should normally be rejected.
- Only consider ANÜ if the technical fit is exceptionally strong and the role would otherwise be unusually attractive.
- Reject bachelor's/master's thesis positions, working-student jobs and internships.
- Only professional positions are being sought.

## Location

- Strong preference for Berlin and the surrounding area.
- Relocation should only be considered for exceptionally good roles.
- Daily commuting is acceptable only when realistic rush-hour travel time is below 40 minutes.
- If on-site presence is required no more than twice per week, up to 60 minutes of travel time can be acceptable.
- Hamburg may be acceptable in some cases, but should be flagged for manual review rather than automatically treated as a good location.

## Compensation and vacation

- Total compensation below EUR 50,000 is normally unacceptable.
- If compensation is uncertain and the role is an exceptional technical fit, do not automatically reject it solely because the estimate may be below this threshold; flag it for manual review instead.
- Collective bargaining agreements are a positive factor.
- 30 vacation days are required; fewer than 30 are not acceptable when the number is explicitly stated.

## Company culture

- The company should appear to value and respect its employees.
- Explicit statements that glorify constant overtime, "going the extra mile" as a normal expectation, or performing under sustained pressure are strong negative signals.

# Particularly interesting technical areas

## Coffee-machine control software

Very strong positive factor.

Examples:

- controlling the coffee grinder,
- reading and monitoring sensors,
- safe control of physical devices and actuators,
- control engineering, especially temperature control.

Less interesting within the same product:

- Wi-Fi interfaces,
- end-user interface development.

## AI-enabled machines

Interesting when AI is part of a real technical system.

Positive examples:

- processing sensor data,
- process control,
- integrating AI into physical products or machines.

Less interesting:

- pure model optimization,
- hyperparameter tuning as the main job content.

## Robotics / autonomous systems

Very attractive.

Positive signals:

- ROS / ROS2,
- sensor integration,
- software that interacts with real physical systems.

## Physics-based software

Positive examples:

- physical modeling,
- simulation,
- FEM-related software.

Important constraint:

- The position should still contain substantial software development. A pure physicist/research role without meaningful software engineering is not a good fit.

Less attractive:

- pure solver development without a clear system/application context.

---

# Less suitable technical directions

## Pure software engineering without a technical application

Usually less attractive:

- web development,
- CRUD applications,
- generic business software,
- pure cloud/backend infrastructure,
- framework development without a technical/physical application,
- driver development as the main focus.

## GUI/UI and website development

- Product application development is generally less interesting than technical/engineering software.
- GUI development is fine for small internal helper tools, but not desirable as the main product-development focus.
- No interest in web design or web application development.

## Technologies/tasks to avoid

Undesirable as a major part of the role:

- LabVIEW,
- SAP,
- SQL/database-centered work.

---

# Purpose / societal value

A positive real-world contribution is an important factor.

Especially positive:

- better coffee,
- sustainability,
- environmental technology,
- education,
- technologies that help people or solve concrete real-world problems.

Ideal combination:

> Interesting technical development work + a useful or meaningful application.

Technical fit remains a prerequisite. A socially useful organization does not automatically make a role attractive if the daily work is mostly coordination, management or otherwise uninteresting.

Negative examples:

- pure profit optimization without a clear benefit,
- advertising/marketing optimization,
- financial products,
- business models with questionable societal value.

# Preferred work style

## Very good fit

- deep technical analysis,
- systematically understanding complex problems,
- working for longer periods within one technical domain,
- changing engineering challenges within a stable context,
- developing concrete solutions,
- using documentation, IDE navigation, diagrams and structured analysis.

Ideal:

> Stable technical context + varied engineering problems.

## Poor fit

Negative factors:

- many parallel projects,
- frequent context switching,
- work dominated by meetings and coordination,
- project management,
- Product Owner roles,
- requirements-only positions,
- technical leadership positions with little hands-on development,
- consulting with constantly changing topics.

Customer contact is acceptable when technical development clearly remains the main activity.

# Evaluation priorities

1. Ethical fit / no clearly harmful application.
2. Technical motivation and actual day-to-day work.
3. Suitable work style.
4. Purpose and societal value.
5. Technical growth potential.
6. Skill fit.
7. Location / remote possibilities.
8. Compensation and contract conditions.

Missing skills are acceptable when they are reasonably learnable and the overall technical direction is a very strong fit.

When important information is missing from a vacancy, flag the uncertainty instead of assuming the best or worst case.
